How to Order

Follow these simple steps to order your food from the cafeteria.

Step 1: Choose Your Items

Browse the menu and select the food and drinks you want.

Step 2: Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your order and pay the total amount.

Step 3: Collect Your Token

Collect your order token or receipt and keep it safe.

Step 4: Pick Up Your Order

Wait for your token number to be called and collect your food from the pickup counter.

Important Guidelines

  • Avoid peak hours if possible.
  • Keep your token until you receive your order.
  • Stand in the queue and wait patiently.
  • Listen for your token number.
  • Check your order before leaving the counter.
  • Keep the pickup area clear.
